## What is being learned

Statistics and probability as they are actually used in AI engineering — starting
with the Week 3 / Class 2 syllabus: normal distribution, Z-scores, frequentist vs
Bayesian probability, and model confidence scores.

## Why (inferred)

The workspace is the **Job Ready AI Bootcamp** repo — a 12-week course with lecture
notebooks and projects per class. The stated need was to have
`week 3/Class 2 Project/week3_class7_project.ipynb` explained *in simple Bangla, at a
15-year-old's level*.

That request implies one of two missions, which change what future lessons should look like:

1. **Teaching mission** — the user is delivering this bootcamp to Bangla-speaking
   students and needs explanations simple enough to hand to a learner.
   → Lessons should be handout-shaped, printable, reusable in class.
2. **Learning mission** — the user is working through the material themselves and
   Bangla at a low reading level is the fastest route to intuition.
   → Lessons should be practice-heavy, tuned to their own zone of proximal development.

Lessons 0001–0005 were written to serve both: simple Bangla, printable, with retrieval
practice built in.

## Grounding constraints (already known)

- **Language:** Bangla prose, 15-year-old reading level.
- **Technical terms stay in English** (Z-score, prior, posterior, calibration) — these
  are the words in the notebooks, the interview room, and the documentation.
- **Every lesson ties back to a notebook section** in this repo, so the lesson and the
  code stay in sync.

## Open question for the user

Which of the two missions above is it — teaching others, or learning it yourself?
