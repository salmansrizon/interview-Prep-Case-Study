# Clinic Hours and Contact

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Opening hours
Greenfield Community Clinic is open Saturday to Thursday from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm. The clinic is closed on Fridays. The clinic pharmacy keeps the same hours, and the laboratory takes blood samples from 8:00 am to 12:00 noon.

## Contacting the clinic
Call the front desk number printed on your clinic card, or visit reception in person. The front desk can book appointments, answer questions about clinic services and put you through to the nurse triage line. Please do not send medical details or photos by social media messages.

## Public holidays and after hours
On public holidays the clinic opens from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm for urgent same-day appointments only. When the clinic is closed, go to the nearest hospital emergency department for urgent problems, or call 999 in an emergency.
