# Headaches

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Common types of headache
The most common headache is a tension-type headache, which feels like a band of pressure around the head. Other common causes are colds, dehydration, missed meals, poor sleep, eye strain and stress. Migraines are less common, often one-sided and may come with nausea or sensitivity to light.

## Self-care for headaches
Drink water, eat regular meals, rest in a quiet, dark room, take breaks from screens and try to keep a regular sleep pattern. A pharmacy pain reliever can help occasionally; follow the label.

## Medication-overuse headache
Taking pain relievers for headaches on many days each month can itself cause more frequent headaches. If you need pain relief for headaches often, book an appointment instead of increasing your use.

## Headache warning signs
Get emergency care for a sudden, very severe headache, often described as the worst ever, or a headache with a stiff neck, fever and rash, confusion, weakness, trouble speaking, or after a head injury. Book an appointment for headaches that are new and keep getting worse.
