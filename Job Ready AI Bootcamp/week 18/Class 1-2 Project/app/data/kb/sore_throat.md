# Sore Throat

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Common causes of a sore throat
Most sore throats are caused by viruses, often with a cold, and get better within about a week without antibiotics. A smaller number are caused by bacteria, such as strep throat, which a clinician can test for.

## Home care for a sore throat
Drink plenty of fluids, rest, and try warm drinks or cold foods to soothe the throat. Adults can gargle with warm salty water. Throat lozenges can help older children and adults but are a choking risk for young children.

## When to see a clinician about a sore throat
Book an appointment if the sore throat lasts more than a week, if you have a high fever, white patches on the tonsils with swollen neck glands, or if it is very painful to swallow. Get emergency help if you cannot swallow your saliva, are drooling, or have difficulty breathing.
