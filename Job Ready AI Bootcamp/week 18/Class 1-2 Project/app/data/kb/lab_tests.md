# Blood Tests and Results

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Preparing for a blood test
Some blood tests, such as a fasting glucose or cholesterol test, need you to stop eating for a set time beforehand. Follow the exact instructions you were given when the test was booked. Unless told otherwise, keep drinking water and take your usual medicines.

## Getting your test results
Most routine results are ready within 3 working days. A clinician reviews every result first. The clinic will contact you if anything needs action; otherwise you can view results in the patient portal or ask at your next appointment.

## Understanding your results
Test results need to be read together with your symptoms, history and other results, so only a clinician can explain what your results mean for you. Book an appointment or a telehealth visit to go through them.
