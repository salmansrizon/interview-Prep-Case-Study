# Dengue

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## What dengue is
Dengue is a viral infection spread by the bite of infected Aedes mosquitoes, which mostly bite during the day. Cases rise during and after the rainy season. Most people recover within one to two weeks, but some develop severe dengue, which needs hospital care.

## Symptoms of dengue
Dengue usually starts with a high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, nausea and sometimes a rash. Anyone with these symptoms during dengue season should contact the clinic for advice and possible testing.

## Care at home for dengue
Rest and drink plenty of fluids such as water and oral rehydration solution. Paracetamol is commonly used for fever and pain in dengue; follow the label or your clinician's advice. Avoid ibuprofen and aspirin unless a clinician tells you otherwise, because they can increase the risk of bleeding.

## Dengue warning signs
The danger period is often around the time the fever goes down. Seek emergency care for severe stomach pain, repeated vomiting, bleeding from the gums or nose, blood in vomit or stool, fast breathing, extreme tiredness or restlessness, or cold, clammy skin.

## Preventing mosquito bites
Remove standing water around the home at least once a week, cover water containers, use mosquito nets and screens, wear long sleeves and use an insect repellent as directed on the label. Protecting a person who has dengue from bites helps stop it spreading to others.
