# Prescriptions and Refills

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Repeat prescriptions
If you take a regular medicine, request a repeat prescription at least 5 working days before you run out, at the front desk, through the patient portal or during a telehealth visit. Some medicines need a review appointment before they can be repeated.

## Lost prescriptions or medicines
If you lose a prescription or your medicines, contact the front desk. A clinician will decide whether a replacement can be issued; for some medicines a new appointment is needed.

## Questions about your medicine
For questions about how to take a medicine, side effects or whether two medicines can be taken together, ask the clinic pharmacist or your clinician. They can check your record and other medicines, which this handbook cannot do.
