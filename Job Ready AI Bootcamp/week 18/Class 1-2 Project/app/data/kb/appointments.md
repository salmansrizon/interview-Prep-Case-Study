# Appointments

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Booking an appointment
Book an appointment at the front desk, by calling the number on your clinic card, or through the patient portal. Routine appointments are usually available within a week. Same-day appointments are kept for problems that should not wait.

## What to bring to an appointment
Bring your clinic card, a photo ID, your medicine list or the medicine boxes themselves, any home readings such as blood pressure or blood sugar, and your child's vaccination card for child visits. Write down your questions before you come.

## Cancelling or arriving late
If you cannot attend, please cancel at least 24 hours before so the slot can go to someone else. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, you may be asked to rebook, unless the problem is urgent.

## Walk-in visits
A limited number of walk-in slots are available each morning from 8:00 am for urgent problems. Patients are seen in order of medical need, not arrival time, so waiting times can vary.
