# Healthy Lifestyle

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Physical activity
Adults are generally advised to aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, such as brisk walking or cycling, spread over most days, plus muscle-strengthening activity on two days. Any activity is better than none. Start slowly and ask a clinician first if you have a heart condition or other long-term illness.

## Healthy eating
Eat plenty of vegetables, fruit, pulses and whole grains, choose fish, lean meat or beans for protein, and limit salt, sugar, sugary drinks and fried foods. Smaller portions of rice and more vegetables on the plate help with weight and blood sugar.

## Tobacco and betel quid
Smoking and chewing tobacco or betel quid (paan with tobacco) cause cancer, heart disease and lung disease. Stopping at any age brings health benefits. The clinic offers support to help you quit; ask at your next appointment.

## Sleep
Most adults need about 7 to 9 hours of sleep a night. A regular bedtime, a dark and quiet room, and avoiding screens and caffeine late in the day help. Talk to a clinician if you snore loudly and feel very sleepy during the day.
