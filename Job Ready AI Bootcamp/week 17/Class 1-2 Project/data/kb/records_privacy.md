# Medical Records and Privacy

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## What the clinic keeps in your record
Your medical record includes your contact details, visit notes, test results, prescriptions, vaccinations and letters. It is stored securely and used only for your care and for running the clinic safely.

## Who can see your record
Only the clinicians and staff involved in your care can open your record, and every access is logged. The clinic does not share your record with family members, employers or anyone else without your written consent, except where the law requires it.

## Requesting a copy of your records
To get a copy of your medical records, fill in a records request form at the front desk and show a photo ID. Copies are usually ready within 7 working days. A parent or legal guardian can request records for a child.

## Your medical record number
Every patient has a medical record number (MRN) that starts with GCC followed by six digits. Keep it private, like a password. Clinic staff will never ask for your MRN by social media or text message.

## About the clinic assistant
The clinic assistant answers general questions from this handbook only. It cannot see your medical record, cannot book appointments and does not keep your questions after answering. Please do not type your name, phone number, ID numbers or record number into it.
