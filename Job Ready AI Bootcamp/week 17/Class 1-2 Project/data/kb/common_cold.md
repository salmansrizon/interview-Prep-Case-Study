# Common Cold

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## What a cold is
A common cold is a viral infection of the nose and throat. Typical symptoms are a runny or blocked nose, sneezing, a mild sore throat, a cough and sometimes a low fever. Most colds get better on their own within 7 to 10 days, although a cough can last longer. Antibiotics do not work against cold viruses.

## Home care for a cold
Rest, drink plenty of fluids and eat what you can. Warm drinks, honey in warm water (not for babies under one year) and saline nose drops can ease symptoms. Wash your hands often and cover coughs and sneezes to protect others. If you use a cold remedy from a pharmacy, read the label and check that you are not taking the same active ingredient twice.

## When to see a clinician about a cold
Book an appointment if symptoms last longer than 10 days, if you feel better and then get worse again, if you have ear pain, or if you have a long-term condition such as asthma, diabetes or heart disease and your symptoms are getting worse. Get urgent help for difficulty breathing.
