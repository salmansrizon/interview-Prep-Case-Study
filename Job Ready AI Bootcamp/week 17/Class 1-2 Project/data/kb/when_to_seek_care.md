# Choosing the Right Care

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Self-care at home
Many minor illnesses, such as colds, mild sore throats, small cuts and minor burns, can be looked after at home with rest, fluids and advice from a pharmacist. Watch for warning signs and seek care if symptoms get worse or do not improve.

## Same-day appointment
Ask for a same-day appointment for problems that are not emergencies but should not wait, for example a fever that lasts more than 3 days, ear pain, pain when passing urine, a wound that looks infected, or a long-term condition that is getting worse.

## Emergency care
Emergencies include chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke signs, severe bleeding, a severe allergic reaction, unconsciousness, a seizure and fever in a baby under 3 months. For these, call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department.

## If you are not sure where to go
If you are unsure how urgent a problem is, call the clinic front desk during opening hours and ask for the nurse triage line. Outside opening hours, if you think it might be an emergency, treat it as one and call 999.
