# Telehealth Visits

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## What telehealth is good for
Telehealth visits are video or phone appointments with a clinician. They work well for follow-ups, reviewing test results, repeat prescriptions, mental health check-ins and simple problems that do not need a physical examination. Telehealth runs Sunday to Thursday from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

## When telehealth is not suitable
Telehealth is not for emergencies. Chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke signs or a very unwell child need emergency care, so call 999. Problems that need an examination or a test may need an in-person visit.

## Preparing for a telehealth visit
Book a telehealth slot through the front desk or the patient portal. Before the call, charge your phone or computer, find a quiet room with good light and internet, and have your medicine list and any home readings ready.

## Privacy during a telehealth visit
Join from a private place where others cannot overhear. The clinic uses an encrypted video service and does not record visits. Do not join from a shared computer you do not trust, and log out when the visit ends.
