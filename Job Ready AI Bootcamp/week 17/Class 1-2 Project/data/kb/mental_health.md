# Mental Health and Wellbeing

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Stress, low mood and anxiety
Everyone feels stressed, low or worried at times. When these feelings last more than two weeks, or make it hard to sleep, work, study or look after yourself, it is a good time to talk to someone. Mental health problems are common and treatable.

## Looking after your wellbeing
Regular sleep, physical activity, time outdoors, eating regular meals and staying in touch with people you trust all support mental wellbeing. Try to limit alcohol and avoid using other substances to cope.

## Counselling at the clinic
Greenfield Community Clinic offers confidential counselling appointments with a trained counsellor. You can ask for one at the front desk or during any appointment. You do not need a referral, and what you share is kept private within your care team.

## In a mental health crisis
If you or someone else is thinking about suicide or self-harm, or is in immediate danger, call 999 now or go to the nearest emergency department. Stay with the person if it is safe, and remove anything they could use to hurt themselves.
