# Preventing Infections

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## When to wash your hands
Wash your hands before preparing or eating food, after using the toilet or changing a nappy, after coughing, sneezing or blowing your nose, after touching animals or rubbish, and before and after caring for someone who is sick.

## How to wash your hands
Wet your hands with clean water, apply soap and rub all surfaces, including the backs of the hands, between the fingers and under the nails, for at least 20 seconds. Rinse and dry with a clean towel. Use an alcohol-based hand rub when soap and water are not available.

## Covering coughs and wearing masks
Cover coughs and sneezes with a tissue or the inside of your elbow, throw tissues away and wash your hands. If you have a cough or fever and must be around other people, a well-fitting mask helps protect them. Please wear a mask in the clinic waiting room if you have a cough.
