# Storing and Disposing of Medicines

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## Storing medicines
Store medicines in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, in their original packaging with the label. A bathroom is usually too damp. Some medicines, such as insulin or certain liquids, need a refrigerator; the label or pharmacist will tell you.

## Keeping children safe from medicines
Keep all medicines, including vitamins and iron tablets, up high and out of sight and reach of children, ideally in a locked box. Close child-resistant caps properly after each use and never call medicine sweets.

## Disposing of unused or expired medicines
Do not use medicines after their expiry date. Bring unused or expired medicines to the clinic pharmacy for safe disposal. Do not throw them in household rubbish or flush them down the toilet unless the label says to.

## Keeping a medicine list
Keep an up-to-date list of every medicine you take, including OTC products, vitamins and herbal remedies, with the strength and how often you take it. Bring the list to every appointment and show it to the pharmacist when you buy something new.
