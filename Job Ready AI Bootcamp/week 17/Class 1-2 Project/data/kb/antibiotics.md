# Antibiotics

> Educational, fictional clinic handbook; not medical advice.

## What antibiotics treat
Antibiotics treat infections caused by bacteria. They do not work against viruses, so they do not help colds, flu, most sore throats or most coughs. A clinician decides whether an antibiotic is needed.

## Taking antibiotics as prescribed
Take an antibiotic exactly as your clinician or pharmacist explains, for as long as you were told, even if you start to feel better. Never share antibiotics, use leftovers from an earlier illness, or buy them without a prescription.

## Antibiotic resistance
Using antibiotics when they are not needed helps bacteria become resistant, which means the medicines stop working when people really need them. Using them only when prescribed protects you and your community.

## Side effects and antibiotic allergy
Common side effects include an upset stomach and diarrhoea. Tell the clinic about any rash or reaction. Swelling of the face or throat, or difficulty breathing after a dose, is an emergency. Always tell clinicians and pharmacists if you have ever had an allergy to an antibiotic.
